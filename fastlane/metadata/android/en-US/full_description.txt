LogSleuth is a root-free Android logging toolkit with two modes.

Viewer app: a polished live logcat viewer with level coloring, filters
(level/tag/keyword/regex/exclusion), saved filter presets, per-app filtering
(via Shizuku), background recording sessions, crash & ANR detection with
notifications, a floating bookmark bubble, and export to txt/zip with device
info.

Embedded SDK: developers can integrate logsleuth-sdk into their own app to
record its logs, crashes and ANRs with zero permissions and zero network. End
users share a log zip in two taps.

Reading other apps' logs requires a one-time grant: either Shizuku (no root;
on Android 11+ it can be activated on-device via Wireless Debugging) or a
one-time ADB command: adb shell pm grant io.github.logsleuth.app
android.permission.READ_LOGS. The embedded SDK mode needs no grant at all.

Open source (Apache-2.0). No ads, no tracking, no network permission.
